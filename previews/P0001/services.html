Cleaning Services

Every visit follows the same written checklist, so quality doesn't depend on who shows up. Choose a one-time clean or a recurring plan.

Most popular

Residential Cleaning

Standard and deep cleans for houses, condos, and apartments. Recurring plans (weekly, biweekly, monthly) get a lower per-visit rate than one-time cleans.

  • Kitchens, bathrooms, living areas, and bedrooms
  • Dusting, vacuuming, mopping, and surface sanitizing
  • Optional add-ons: inside oven, inside fridge, interior windows

Commercial & Office Cleaning

After-hours or early-morning cleaning for small offices and commercial spaces, scheduled to avoid disrupting your team or customers.

  • Common areas, restrooms, break rooms, and workstations
  • Trash removal and restocking of consumables (by arrangement)
  • Flexible recurring schedules to match your business hours

Move-In / Move-Out & Deep Cleaning

A thorough, top-to-bottom clean for move-ins, move-outs, and seasonal resets — the kind of clean a standard recurring visit doesn't cover.

  • Baseboards, inside cabinets, light fixtures, and vents
  • Appliance interiors and behind/under furniture where accessible
  • Recommended before a move-in, after a move-out, or once or twice a year

Recurring Maintenance Plans

Weekly, biweekly, or monthly visits on a consistent schedule, with the option to pause or reschedule with notice.

Specific plan pricing is not published on this demo site — every plan is quoted after a short property questionnaire.
